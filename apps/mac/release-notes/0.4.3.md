After iPhone restarts, the app now finds it again and tells you to unlock it.
If iPhone does not come back, the screen has a Check Again button.
The app makes iPhone forget this Mac, so after the restart iPhone asks to trust it again.
Unlock iPhone and tap Trust. The app then pairs with iPhone again and goes on. You no longer need to unplug the cable.
The fast method is now the only method. The full copy is gone, so you no longer need a backup password or free space for a copy.
The app does not support iOS 27 yet. For an iPhone on iOS 27, the app shows the manual guide with Apple Configurator instead.
The app moves the copy of iPhone that older versions kept on the Mac to the Trash. Empty the Trash to free the space.
