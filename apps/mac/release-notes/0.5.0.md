The app now supervises an iPhone on iOS 27 and does not erase it.
On iOS 27 the iPhone restarts by itself during the run. The window tells you what to do on the iPhone at each step.
We tested this on one iPhone SE (2nd generation) with iOS 27.2. Back up your iPhone first.
After the run, the iPhone may ask for your Apple Account password once.
iOS 28 and later are not supported yet.
