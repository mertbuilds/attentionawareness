The app is now free. You do not need a key.

- Back up your iPhone first, with Finder or iCloud. The app asks you to confirm this.
- Full copy is the default method. It works on every iOS version.
- The fast method is experimental. It is only offered on iOS 26 and older.
- When a supervision finishes, the app sends one anonymous count. It holds the app version, the method, and the first number of the iOS version and of the macOS version. Nothing that names you or your iPhone.
- If the app helps you, you can support the work or share it. The last screen has both buttons.
- The app is open source now.
