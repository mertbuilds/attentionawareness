If Screen Time is not working for you, the cause is usually not a bug. Screen Time lets the person who set a limit skip that limit, and on your own iPhone that person is you. A limit holds only when you cannot remove it yourself.

I am Mert. I make attention awareness. My screen time went from more than 5 hours a day to 1 hour 45 minutes. I tried a lot of tools on the way. The one that stuck is the last one in this post.

## Quick fixes to try first

1. **Let another person set the passcode.** Ask them to choose the code and to enter their own Apple Account for Screen Time Passcode Recovery. Then your Apple Account cannot reset the code.
2. **Remove the app from Always Allowed.** Apps on that list work at all times, whatever your schedule and limits say. In Settings, open Screen Time, then Always Allowed, tap Edit and remove the app.
3. **Make the schedule block, not remind.** On iOS 26 and earlier, open Screen Time, then Downtime, and turn on Block at Downtime. Without it, the phone only reminds you, and you can keep using the apps. This option needs a Screen Time passcode.
4. **Limit the app's website too.** On iOS 27, a Time Allowance also counts the app's website when the app declares one. On iOS 26 and earlier, open Content & Privacy Restrictions > App Store, Media, Web, & Games > Web Content, choose Limit Adult Websites, then add the website under Never Allow.
5. **Use the same limits on your iPad and Mac.** In Screen Time, open Manage Screen Time and turn on Share Across Devices. Before iOS 27, the switch is on the Screen Time page itself. Your limits and schedules then apply on every device signed in to your Apple Account.

If these do not hold, here is why, and what does.

## What Screen Time is good for

Screen Time is free and already on your iPhone. It does three jobs well.

- **It shows your numbers.** Go to Settings > Screen Time. You see your daily average, your most used apps and your pickups. Start here.
- **It sets light limits.** If a reminder makes you stop, you do not need anything stronger.
- **It works better for a child.** A parent sets the passcode and the child does not know it. See [iPhone parental controls kids cannot turn off](/blog/iphone-parental-controls-kids-cannot-turn-off) for that setup and its weak points.

## How to set Screen Time up as strongly as it goes

Apple renamed parts of Screen Time in iOS 27. I use the iOS 27 names and give the old name the first time.

1. Open Settings and tap Screen Time.
2. If Screen Time is off, turn it on. Apple calls this App & Website Activity.
3. Set a daily limit. Tap Time Allowances (called App Limits before iOS 27). Before iOS 27, tap Add Limit next.
4. Set hours with no apps. Tap Screen Time Schedule (called Downtime before iOS 27) and turn the schedule on.
5. Set a passcode. Tap Manage Screen Time, then Lock Screen Time Settings, and enter a four-digit code.
6. On iOS 26 and earlier, make the schedule block. Tap Downtime and turn on Block at Downtime. Apple says limits can be ignored by default, and this is the setting it gives to stop that.

Even then, Screen Time has four ways out.

## Why Screen Time limits fail for adults

### 1. The Ignore Limit button

Apple's guide for iOS 26 says: "By default, Screen Time limits can be ignored once reached." When your time is over, the screen shows an Ignore Limit button. You can ask for a little more time, or ignore the limit for the whole day.

### 2. You know your own passcode

A passcode removes the Ignore Limit button. But you chose the code. Some people ask a friend to set the code. That helps, until the next point.

### 3. The passcode can be reset with your Apple Account

In Settings, open Screen Time, then Manage Screen Time, and tap Change Screen Time Passcode. Choose the option for a forgotten passcode. The iPhone asks for the Apple Account used for recovery, then lets you set a new code. So a friend's code holds only if the recovery account is also not yours.

### 4. You can turn it all off

With the passcode, open Manage Screen Time in Screen Time and turn off App & Website Activity. Every limit and schedule stops.

## Why willpower loses at 11 p.m.

You set the limit in the morning, when you are rested and you have a plan. The limit screen shows up at 11 p.m., when you are tired and the video is half finished. The button asks the tired person, not the person who made the plan.

This is not weakness. A rule that asks for a new decision every night will lose some nights. The fix is to decide once, at a good moment, and make it hard to change later.

## The only setup with no button to press

For the iPhones they hand out, schools and companies use supervision. A supervised iPhone can hold a profile that nobody can remove in Settings. There is no Ignore Limit button and no passcode.

You can do this on your own iPhone in two free ways.

- **The free Mac app.** [attention awareness](/) supervises your iPhone without erasing it, on iOS 27 and earlier. It sends a small set of settings to the iPhone and restarts it. Your photos and apps stay. Back up your iPhone first, with Finder or iCloud. Then it installs a profile that hides the apps you choose and blocks the websites you choose. No account, no app on the iPhone, and nobody can see or track the phone.
- **Manually via Apple Configurator.** [The manual guide](/guide) does the same with Apple Configurator. It erases the iPhone first. Only what syncs to iCloud comes back.

The profile can also turn on Apple's adult website filter. See [how to block adult websites on an iPhone](/blog/block-adult-websites-iphone) and [how to turn an iPhone into a dumbphone](/blog/turn-iphone-into-dumbphone).

What it takes:

- You need a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later, and a cable.
- The Mac app works on iOS 27 and earlier. On iOS 27 the steps on the iPhone differ a little. They are in [how to supervise an iPhone on iOS 27 without erasing it](/blog/supervise-iphone-ios-27-without-erasing).
- It takes a few minutes.
- If you lock the profile, you cannot undo it on the iPhone itself. That takes erasing the iPhone, or a Mac with Apple Configurator.

In trial mode you can remove the profile in Settings. Use it for a week before you lock anything.

## What this cannot do

- It does not work on Android or Windows. It is for iPhone only today.
- It does not set time limits. A hidden app is hidden all day.
- It does not block everything. Apple's adult filter is a filter and it misses some websites.
- It is not a medical treatment.

## Which option fits you

- **You want to know your numbers:** Screen Time. Stop there if a reminder is enough.
- **You ignore limits sometimes:** Screen Time with a passcode that a friend sets, or ScreenZen or one sec.
- **You ignore limits most nights:** Opal's strictest setting or Brick.
- **You have removed every blocker you tried:** a supervised iPhone with a locked profile.
- **You are setting up a child's iPhone:** parental Screen Time first, then supervision if your child finds a way around it.

## Questions people ask

### Why is Screen Time not working on my iPhone?

In most cases the limit works as Apple designed it. By default a limit can be ignored when it is reached. Set a Screen Time passcode to remove that button. If limits disappear, update iOS and set them again.

### How do I stop ignoring Screen Time limits?

Set a Screen Time passcode, so the limit screen asks for a code. Ask someone you trust to choose the code and the recovery account. If you still get past it, use a setup that has no code, such as a supervised iPhone.

### Can I remove the Ignore Limit button?

Yes. Set a Screen Time passcode. On iOS 26 and earlier, also turn on Block at Downtime, which Apple gives as the way to stop limits being ignored. The limit screen then asks for the passcode, but anyone who knows the code can still get in.

### Do I have to erase my iPhone to supervise it?

Not with the free Mac app, on iOS 27 and earlier. It sends a small set of settings to the iPhone and restarts it, so your apps and photos stay. Back up first anyway. Doing it manually via Apple Configurator does erase the iPhone.

## Start with the trial

If Screen Time is enough for you, keep it. If you tap Ignore Limit most nights, [get the free Mac app](/) and run the trial mode for a week. If you prefer to do each step yourself, [follow the manual guide](/guide).
