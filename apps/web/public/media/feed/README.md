# Feed clips

Eleven portrait clips for the phone on the homepage, `01.mp4` to `11.mp4`,
with a poster frame each, `01.jpg` to `11.jpg`. Clip 01 is hour 2 and clip 11
is hour 12. The phone swipes from one to the next, and after clip 11 it starts
again at clip 01.

720 by 1560 (or 1080 by 2340), H.264 mp4, 2 to 5 seconds, loops, under 2 MB.
The site plays them muted. A missing clip shows a plain wash instead.
